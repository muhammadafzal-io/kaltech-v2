situation:
  - Blood bank operations ran on paper forms and spreadsheets. Donor records, screening results, component inventory and issuance were each recorded, but in separate places and mostly after the event.
  - The brief asked for digitisation. Take what is on paper and put it on a screen.

constraint:
  figure: Process
  figureLabel: The binding constraint
  paras:
    - Compliance checkpoints were being recorded after the fact rather than enforced during the work. A screening result written up at the end of a shift documents that a check happened. It does not prevent a unit advancing before the check is complete.
    - Digitising that faithfully produces a faster version of the same exposure. The question was not how to record the work. It was whether the work could be specified tightly enough that the system could refuse to let it happen out of order.

setAside:
  - heading: Digitise the forms
    body: What was briefed. Delivers a searchable archive and changes nothing about the risk.
    taken: false
  - heading: Buy an off-the-shelf blood bank system
    body: Viable in principle. The available options assumed single-site operation and would not hold a multi-branch network under one inventory view.
    taken: false
  - heading: Encode the compliance sequence
    body: The path taken. Build the workflow so that ISBT 128 requirements are gates in the process rather than fields on a record.
    taken: true

built:
  - num: "01"
    heading: Donor lifecycle management
    body: Registration, history, eligibility and deferral tracking held as one record across branches, so a donor deferred at one site is deferred at all of them.
  - num: "02"
    heading: Automated screening workflow
    body: Screening as an enforced sequence. A unit cannot progress to component processing until the required results exist and pass.
  - num: "03"
    heading: Component inventory, live
    body: Stock by component type, group and expiry across the branch network, visible in one place.
  - num: "04"
    heading: Issuance and audit tracking
    body: Every unit traceable from donor to recipient, with the audit trail generated by the work rather than assembled for an inspection.
  - num: "05"
    heading: Multi-branch control
    body: One operating picture across sites, with branch-level permissions and a single inventory view above them.

difference:
  - Treating compliance as a property of the workflow rather than a report produced from it. That distinction is the difference between a system that documents what happened and one that constrains what can happen. In regulated operations it is the only version worth building.
