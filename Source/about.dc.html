KalTech is an applied AI and software firm. We work with established mid-market companies on systems that have to hold up in production, in front of customers, and under audit.

The firm

What we actually do.

We are not a venture studio, not a staff augmentation shop, and not an agency that ships a prototype and invoices for the learning. We diagnose the constraint a business is operating under, scope the work against it, and build the system that moves it. The same people do all three.

Most of what we build sits in environments where failure is expensive. Regulated lending, cross-border logistics, hospital operations. That is the work we are best at, and it is the reason our first question is always about the process rather than the model.
