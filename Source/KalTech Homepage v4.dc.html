Our Method

Most companies commission a solution before the constraint has been identified.

A brief specifies a solution that has already been chosen. It rarely establishes that the solution addresses the constraint the business is actually operating under. KalTech identifies that constraint first, across process, data and economics.

01 Process

Is this a process, or a habit?

Software applied to undefined work produces an unreadable result. We establish whether the work, and the systems it currently runs through, are specified tightly enough to be encoded before anything is scoped.

We ask: Could two people perform this work and produce the same result?

02 Data

Would two systems agree on this number?

Most integration failures are definitional rather than technical. We establish whether the business holds one definition of a customer, an order or a case, or several definitions that disagree.

We ask: Could two systems answer the same question and return the same number?

03 Unit Economics

Does the build clear its own cost at this volume?

A system carries a maintenance cost that is rarely priced at scoping. We establish the volume at which it pays for itself, and whether the business operates above that line.

We ask: What does this cost to run in year two, and at what volume does it clear it?

The Diagnostic

Every engagement begins with a diagnostic.

The diagnostic runs against the three lenses. It ends in a written finding and a scope priced against it. Companies that proceed to a build do so with the constraint already established. Companies that do not proceed keep the finding.

How the diagnostic works →

Sometimes the finding is that nothing should be built. We deliver that as readily as any other.

What it returns

1The constraint that is binding, stated plainly, and the evidence for it.
2What that constraint currently costs the business, quantified.
3What can be built against it, sequenced by payback rather than ambition.
4The line in the P&L the build is expected to move, and the volume at which it does.

What it is not

This is not a sales document. Often the answer is that the thing you asked for should not be built, and we say so in writing.

Who it is for

Companies with a functioning business and a specific commercial problem.
