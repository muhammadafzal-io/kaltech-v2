Why it exists

A firm that builds should not be trusted to say what needs building.

That is the honest form of the objection, and we do not answer it by promising to be careful. We answer it by charging for the finding as a document rather than as the opening phase of a build, which means the diagnostic covers its own cost whatever it concludes. Where the work recommends less than the client came in asking for, or nothing at all, we have already been paid for the work that established it.

The alternative arrangement, where the assessment is free and the revenue sits entirely in what follows, has one predictable output. Free assessments recommend builds. Not because the people writing them are dishonest, but because a firm that has absorbed the cost of the analysis has an interest in a particular conclusion, and interests are more reliable than intentions.

The diagnostic fee is not credited against a subsequent build. Crediting it would reintroduce the incentive the fee exists to remove.

The three lenses

Process, data, unit economics.

One of the three is usually binding. The diagnostic establishes which, what it costs to leave in place, and what it would take to move. The three are examined in this order because a data problem examined before a process problem returns an answer that is technically correct and operationally useless.

An illustration. Which lens is binding is what the diagnostic establishes, and it is not the same one twice running.

01

Process

Is this a process, or a habit?

Software applied to undefined work produces an unreadable result. It encodes one person's version of the job, presents it as the standard, and removes the discretion that was quietly holding the operation together. We establish whether the work, and the systems it currently runs through, are specified tightly enough to be encoded before anything is scoped.

What we examine

How the work is performed rather than how it is documented. Where the variation between operators sits, which steps carry judgment that has never been written down, and what the people doing the work do when the documented path does not fit the case in front of them.

When this lens is binding

The same input produces materially different outputs depending on who handled it, and nobody in the operation can say with confidence which output was correct.

We ask: Could two people perform this work and produce the same result?

02

Data

Would two systems agree on this number?

Most integration failures are definitional rather than technical. The business holds more than one definition of a customer, an order or a case, no integration reconciles a disagreement that was never settled before the systems were connected, and the disagreement surfaces as a reporting problem long after it became an operating one.

What we examine

Where the same entity is defined in more than one place, what reconciles those definitions today and how often, and how long a disagreement survives before anyone notices it.

When this lens is binding

A question about the business has more than one defensible answer, and which answer gets used depends on which system was opened first.

We ask: Could two systems answer the same question and return the same number?

03

Unit economics

Does the build clear its own cost at this volume?

A system carries a running cost that is rarely priced at scoping. Inference and infrastructure, monitoring, the human review that stays in the loop longer than anyone planned, and the engineering time that maintenance consumes for as long as the system exists. We establish the volume at which the build pays for itself, and whether the business currently operates above that line or below it.

What we examine

Today's volumes rather than forecast ones, the cost of the operation as it currently runs, and what the system would cost to keep running in a year when nobody is excited about it any more.

When this lens is binding

The system works and still does not pay. This is the most expensive of the three failures because it passes every test at launch and only becomes visible in year two, by which point the budget that would have caught it has been spent.

Illustrative. The volume is what the diagnostic calculates.

We ask: What does this cost to run in year two, and at what volume does it clear it?

Access and interviews

We sit with the operators who run the work rather than with the sponsors who described it. The gap between those two accounts is frequently where the constraint is.

02

Evidence

We pull the numbers ourselves rather than accepting the reported ones. Where a figure cannot be reconstructed from something we can see, it does not go in the finding.

The decision has already been made and what is wanted is a document that supports it
